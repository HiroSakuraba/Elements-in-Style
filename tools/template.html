Periodic Spiral

Each turn of the spiral is one period, winding outward from hydrogen. Elements in the same group sit on the same ray. Pick an element to zoom in and watch its electron orbitals pulse.

Scroll or pinch to zoom · drag to pan · ←/→ steps through Z

Lanthanides (La–Yb) and actinides (Ac–No) branch off between groups 2 and 3 into the two outer arcs. Lu and Lr sit in group 3, as in the IUPAC 2021 layout. Element data from the mendeleev database (NIST, NUBASE and CRC sources); values for elements past fermium are mostly predictions.

Drag to rotate. Tap a subshell to isolate it. Lobes are hydrogen-like orbitals scaled by each subshell’s Slater effective charge. Distance from the nucleus is rescaled so the shells sit evenly spaced; filled inner shells are drawn dimmer. Bright and dark lobes have opposite wavefunction sign.
